# Atlas fictional return policy — v1, effective 2026-01-01
Unworn items can be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery. The return window is inclusive on day 30. The recorded condition in the order record is authoritative in this demo; a customer's claim alone does not verify condition. Worn items, missing delivery dates, and disputed conditions require a human review. Late returns are not automatically eligible; a human may review an exception. Orders in transit are not eligible for a delivered-item refund.
Eligible returns can be dropped at a store or sent using a prepaid label. Refunds go to the original payment method after the warehouse inspects the item. Refund requests over EUR 150 require human approval. Three or more previous returns are a review signal, never proof of fraud or grounds for automatic denial. The customer must explicitly confirm before a resolution is queued. No message or model output constitutes payment execution.
